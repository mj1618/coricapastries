Six directions for the new Corica Pastries home page

Each concept below is a working home page. They all keep the Corica logo exactly as it is, use the shop's own photography and copy, and aim for a traditional feel with a modern standard of finish. Open any concept in a new tab and try it on a phone as well as a desktop.

1 Heritage2 Editorial3 Shopfront4 Boutique5 Modern6 Warm Continental
These are home page concepts only. Menu links, product pages, catering and contact pages are placeholders at this stage. Hours, address and phone number are taken from the current site and should be confirmed.
1

Concept oneHeritage

The classic choice. A centred, symmetrical layout in the logo's deep green and cream with fine gold rules, round photo plates and an "Est. 1957" seal. It reads like a fine old pasticceria that has been quietly kept in perfect order.

Cormorant Garamond & EB Garamond

Concept twoEditorial

Warm paper tones, a large Caslon headline and a numbered index of the ranges, in the manner of a well-set food magazine. The most typographic of the set, with a green feature panel for the strudel and a two-column story with a drop cap.

Libre Caslon & Figtree

Concept threeShopfront

The friendliest option, built around the Aberdeen Street shop itself: a striped awning, stacked photo cards, a menu board of the ranges with dotted leaders, trading hours and a map. Green with terracotta accents drawn from the logo.

Concept fourBoutique

Deep Corica green throughout, cream text, gold hairlines and arched photo frames. A refined, gift-box feel that positions Corica as a premium patisserie while keeping every element traditional. A cream section breaks up the green.

Concept fiveModern

The most contemporary of the six: near-white ground, generous space, a photo mosaic and a large Bodoni headline (an Italian typeface, chosen as a quiet nod to the family's roots). Green is used sparingly as the accent. Calm and premium rather than trendy.
