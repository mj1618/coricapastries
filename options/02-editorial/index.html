Our story

Perfected since 1957.

  • 1957Giuseppe Corica establishes Corica Pastries, bringing continental recipes to Perth.
  • NorthbridgeThe Aberdeen Street cake shop becomes a Perth institution.
  • TodayThe same recipes, the same care, baked fresh every morning.

Established in 1957, Corica Pastries has consistently been delighting customers with our world-famous apple strudels, continental and custom-made cakes, and a choice of unique pastries.

Whether you purchase a Corica product from another retailer or in our very own cake shop, you'll never be disappointed. Using recipes perfected since 1957, we offer our customers unique strudels, cakes and pastries.
